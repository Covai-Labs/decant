Welcome to Decant!

Thank you for installing Decant. You are now ready to decant web content into clean Markdown.

🚀 Quick Start Guide

  • Clip Any Webpage: Click the Decant icon in your browser extension toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+M.
  • Copy or Handoff to Obsidian: Use 1-click buttons in the popup to copy Markdown to your clipboard or hand off directly to Obsidian / Logseq.
  • Send to AI Chat: Click 🤖 Send to AI to transfer the decanted article directly to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for automated summaries.
  • Customize Preferences: Open Options to customize YAML frontmatter templates, heading styles, and PKM vault names.

🔒 Privacy Guarantee

Decant operates 100% locally in your browser. Your browsing history, clipped articles, and settings never leave your device.
